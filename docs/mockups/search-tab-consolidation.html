Group the three lookup tabs under one "Search" tab

Search by Name, SKU Lookup, and Scan UPC currently sit as three separate top-level tabs alongside Manual Entry and Import from Website. This groups all three under a single Search tab as a method chooser — every form stays available, just organized one level deeper, and the top bar goes from five tabs to three. Inside the tab, picking Search by Name, SKU Lookup, or Scan UPC comes first, styled the same as the form's other toggle-btn groups rather than a second row of tabs; the Wine / Spirits · Beer toggle sits right below it — set once for whichever method is picked, instead of repeated on each of the three.
